About My Research

I investigate pressure-induced phase transitions, elastic anomalies, phonon instabilities and thermodynamic behaviour in Earth and planetary materials. My work combines first-principles simulations with experimental observations to understand how materials respond to extreme conditions.

Current Research

DFT-based viscosity of liquids

Currently working on first-principles molecular-dynamics calculations to investigate the viscosity and atomic-scale dynamics of liquid materials relevant to deep-Earth and planetary interiors.

DFTAIMDLiquid-state propertiesViscosity

Alloy substitution using DFT

Investigating compositional substitution in materials and solid solutions using DFT, including structural stability and composition-dependent physical properties.

DFTAlloy substitutionSolid solutionsMixing energetics

High-pressure mineral physics

Studying pressure-induced structural phase transitions, equations of state and elastic properties of GeO₂ polymorphs, with implications for understanding Earth materials.

GeO₂High pressureElasticityPhase transitions

Vibrational and thermodynamic properties

Using phonon calculations and quasi-harmonic methods to explore lattice dynamics, thermal properties and the stability of crystalline phases.
